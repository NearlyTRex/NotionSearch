🔍 Welcome to NotionSearch

Let's connect your Notion. This takes about two minutes and you only do it once.

  1. Create an integration

    Open notion.so/my-integrations and click New integration. Give it a name like Search, pick your workspace, and make sure its capabilities include Read content.

  2. Copy the secret

    On the integration's page, find Internal Integration Secret and click Show, then Copy. It starts with ntn_ or secret_. Paste it below.

  3. Share your pages with it

    This is the step people miss. Notion only lets the integration see pages you explicitly share with it. In Notion, open a top-level page, click the ••• menu at the top right, choose Connections → Connect to, and pick your integration. Everything nested inside that page is included automatically, so sharing a few top-level pages usually covers the whole workspace.
