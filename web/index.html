🔍 Welcome to NotionSearch

Let's connect your Notion. This takes about two minutes and you only do it once.

  1. Create a connection

    In Notion, go to Settings → Developer tools → the Connections tab (notion.so/my-integrations also lands there). Click + New connection, name it something like Search, pick your workspace, and make sure it can read content.

    Choose the Access token auth type — the non-OAuth one. OAuth is for apps that many people install; this only needs to reach your own workspace.

  2. Copy its access token

    Open the connection you just made and copy its access token. It starts with ntn_. Paste it below.

    Don't use the Personal access tokens tab. It sits right next to Connections and sounds like the same thing, but it's a different mechanism and won't work here.

  3. Connect your pages to it

    This is the step everyone misses, and nothing works without it. Creating the connection gives it access to nothing. You have to connect pages to it, one time each.

    In Notion, open a page → the ••• menu at the top right → Connections (or Add connections) → pick your connection → confirm.

    Everything nested inside that page comes along automatically, so a few top-level pages usually cover everything. Pages under “Private” must each be connected — owning them isn't enough.
